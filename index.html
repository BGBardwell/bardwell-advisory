About Bardwell Advisory

Experience grounded in operations. Perspective informed by finance.

Bardwell Advisory is an independent consulting firm helping healthcare organizations and construction-related businesses strengthen operational performance, financial visibility, and management decision-making.

Founded by Brian Bardwell, the firm draws on more than 20 years of professional experience spanning healthcare operations, supply chain management, business transformation, and operational finance.

Brian's healthcare experience includes physician practice operations, performance improvement, and supply chain management, including nine years with HCA Healthcare. His subsequent work with Amgen Biopharmaceuticals expanded his experience in strategic transformation, project leadership, organizational change management, and cross-functional stakeholder engagement—supporting enterprise initiatives involving business processes, technology, and operational change.

More recently, Brian served as Controller for a concrete manufacturing business, gaining firsthand experience with the financial and operational challenges facing construction-related companies. His work included management reporting, profitability and cost analysis, cash-flow planning, and financial process improvement.

Drawing on his business architecture expertise, Brian connects financial information, business processes, and operational performance to help organizations gain greater visibility into their operations and make informed decisions.

Bardwell Advisory brings this combined perspective to focused consulting engagements, implementation support, and ongoing financial advisory relationships.
